Privacy Policy &
Data Protection

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Wave Music is architected around a strict local-first, zero-tracking philosophy.

Core Guarantee

We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data, listening history, or created playlists to any central database. Everything stays on your device.

Data Handling Principles

Last updated: August 2026

1. Local Storage & IndexedDB

Wave Music saves your user preferences (such as volume settings, active playlist, liked songs list, and recent song queries) directly in your web browser using standard client-side technologies: localStorage and IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device.

2. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

We do not embed third-party advertising tracking pixels, telemetry scripts, session replays, or invasive analytics tools (such as Facebook Pixel, Google AdSense, or data brokers).

3. Network Audio Requests

When you play a track, your browser connects directly to publicly accessible audio Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) to fetch the audio stream. These requests contain standard HTTP headers required for streaming media but contain no personal identifiers or account credentials.

4. Cookies Policy

Wave Music does not use persistent tracking cookies. Temporary session caching is handled by our Service Worker purely for offline performance and quick application reloads.

5. Clearing Your Data

You have 100% control over your data. You can erase all playlists, history, and favorites at any time by clearing your browser's site data / cache, or clicking "Log Out / Reset" in the Profile Menu.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at: wave.music.app@gmail.com.
