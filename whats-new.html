Changelog & Milestones

Complete history of updates and releases

August 2026 — Latest (v3.0 Beta)

Wave Music 3.0 — The Next Generation

  • Netflix-Style Podcast Hub: High-fidelity horizontal scrollable rows with tag grouping, spotlight billboard hero, and category discovery.
  • Synchronized Karaoke Lyrics: Studio-grade real-time lyric scrolling with animated active phrases and time-sync engine.
  • Wave DNA & Vibe Flow: Interactive mood analyzer and musical persona graph based on your streaming habits.
  • Dynamic Island 2.0: Redesigned compact HUD with live animated audio spectrum, scrubber, and floating controls.
  • Lossless 320kbps JioSaavn CDN: Pure direct CDN audio feeds with zero buffering and instant track switching.
  • Night Wave Mode: Deep OLED black palette with starry ambient animations for late-night listening.
May 2026 — v2.0

Major Architecture Overhaul

  • Zero Extraction Delay: Migrated completely to direct API endpoints, cutting track launch time by 92%.
  • Smart Audio Caching: Next-track prefetching using Service Worker Cache API for gapless transitions.
  • Custom Playlist Engine: Create, reorder, rename, and add custom artwork to unlimited playlists.
  • Full PWA Standalone Support: Offline shell, desktop window controls, and mobile gesture controls.
April 2026 — v1.5

Visual Refresh & Library Upgrades

  • Spotify-Inspired Layout: 3-column grid featuring collapsible sidebar library, ambient main area, and track details strip.
  • Profile Avatar & History: Track your most played songs, recent searches, and favorite artists.
  • Multi-Format Search: Instant live dropdown with predictive suggestions for Hindi, English, Punjabi, and Regional songs.
March 2026 — v1.0

Wave Music Initial Launch

  • Core audio player with volume, shuffle, repeat, and queue capabilities.
  • High-contrast glassmorphism theme and responsive mobile player.
