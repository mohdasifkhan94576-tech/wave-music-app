What is a PWA (Progressive Web App)?

The modern standard for lightweight, installable mobile applications

A Progressive Web App (PWA) gives you all the benefits of a native app without the heavy storage burden. It installs directly from your browser onto your home screen and operates inside a dedicated, standalone fullscreen window without browser URL bars or navigation clutter.

Microscopic Storage (< 5MB)

Traditional music apps consume 150MB to 500MB of storage. Wave PWA takes less than 5MB of space on your phone.

Instant Background Updates

No waiting for Google Play Store updates. When we add new podcast rows or audio codecs, you get them instantly.

Lockscreen & Dynamic Island

Control audio playback, scrub timelines, and skip tracks straight from your phone's lock screen and notification bar.

100% Free & Ad-Free

No intrusive ads, no subscription fees, and no in-app purchases. Pure uninterrupted music streaming.

How to Install Wave Music on Your Device

Quick 10-second setup for Android, iPhone, and Desktop

Android (Chrome / Edge)

Recommended
  1. Open Wave Music in Chrome or your mobile browser.
  2. Tap the Three Dots (⋮) menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap Install. The Wave Music icon will appear on your home screen!

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

iOS 14+
  1. Open Wave Music in the Safari browser.
  2. Tap the Share button (⎋ with arrow) at the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top-right. Launch directly from your home screen!

Windows / Mac / Linux

Desktop Window
  1. Open Wave Music in Chrome, Edge, or Brave.
  2. Click the Install icon in the right side of the URL address bar.
  3. Click Install. Wave will open in its own dedicated, sleek desktop window!
